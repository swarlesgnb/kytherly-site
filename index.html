The mechanism

Three gears that keep every customer turning toward a booked job.

Each one runs on its own the moment something happens. You keep working. Customers hear back right away.

When you can't pick up

Missed-call text-back

Every unanswered call gets a friendly text from your business number within seconds, so the caller has a reason to wait for you instead of dialing someone else.

Hi, this is Sam from Peak Plumbing. Sorry we missed your call, we're probably on a job. How can we help? Reply STOP to opt out. Water heater is leaking in the garage
When a lead comes in

Instant lead reply

Website form and chat leads get a text and email with your booking link right away, and you get an alert. No reply in an hour? One polite follow-up goes out.

Thanks for reaching out about your AC. Is it not cooling at all, or just weak? Not at all. House is 84°
When the job is done

Review requests

When the job is marked done, every customer gets the same short thank-you with a one-tap link to your Google review page. No gating, no gift cards, just a steady climb.

Thanks for having us out today, Dana! Mind leaving a quick review? Done ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

Every text goes out from a registered business number under TCPA and A2P 10DLC rules. STOP and HELP work on every message, nothing is sent before 8 a.m. or after 9 p.m. in your customer's time zone, and we only text people who contacted you first. You approve every message before launch.

Why Kytherly

Named for the oldest known machine with gears.

In 1901, sponge divers pulled a lump of corroded bronze from a shipwreck off the Greek island of Antikythera. Inside were dozens of interlocking gears, built more than 2,000 years ago to track the sun, the moon and the coming eclipses.

Small, precise parts, working together, so people could stop worrying about what came next.

That is what we build for a trade business. You do the work. The mechanism keeps time.
