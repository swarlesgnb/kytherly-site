Here's the rules part. Texts go out from a registered business number and follow TCPA and A2P 10DLC rules. STOP always works. A missed-call reply goes out right away, because that person just called you. Follow-ups and review requests wait until between 8 a.m. and 9 p.m. in your customer's time zone. It only texts people who contacted you first, and you approve every message before anything goes live.
