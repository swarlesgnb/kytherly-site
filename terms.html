Three-month minimum and cancellation

Starter, Growth, and Full Front Desk have a minimum of three monthly service periods, then continue month to month. This minimum does not automatically apply to other products or override a different existing agreement.

You may request cancellation at any time through the cancellation form or by emailing ryleechapin@kytherly.com. For these three packages, cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period once the minimum is complete. A request made during the minimum will be recorded for the end of that minimum; you do not need to submit it again. We confirm the effective date by email. Other services follow their agreed order terms.

Requests are processed manually. You can still use the Stripe billing portal to update payment details and view invoices.

The 60-second guarantee

For the missed-call text-back service advertised on our website: if a missed caller does not get a text back within 60 seconds, day or night, you do not pay for that month. Tell us the affected number, date, and approximate time so we can check the incident and arrange the month's fee adjustment. This is not a promise that every caller will book.
