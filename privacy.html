Privacy policy

Effective October 25, 2026

Kytherly is operated by Rylee Chapin, based in Colorado, United States. This policy covers kytherly.com, inquiries sent to us, cancellation requests, and our billing relationship with customers. Contact ryleechapin@kytherly.com about privacy.

Information you provide

  • Audit requests: name, business name, trade, phone number, and email address. We use these to respond about your requested audit, as stated on the form.
  • Cancellation and support requests: your name, billing email, business name, service, and any message you provide. We use these to identify the relevant account, respond, and process your request.
  • Orders and billing: contact and billing details, purchased services, invoices, subscription status, and payment records used to manage your order, payments, and support.

Please do not put payment card details, passwords, or sensitive personal information in our contact forms.

Services involved

  • Formspree receives and processes website form submissions so we can handle your request. See Formspree's privacy policy.
  • Stripe processes payments and provides checkout, invoices, receipts, and the customer billing portal. Payment credentials are entered on Stripe's pages, not our website forms. We can access the billing and transaction information Stripe makes available to us. See Stripe's privacy policy.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Google Fonts supplies fonts on our homepage. Your browser connects to Google to load those fonts, which exposes connection information such as your IP address. See Google Fonts privacy information.

We also use the correspondence you send by email to answer and maintain a record of your request. Service providers may process information in the United States and other countries under their own terms and privacy notices.

Browser storage

The homepage saves your chosen light or dark theme in your browser's local storage under ky-theme. You can remove it by clearing this site's browser data. The current website does not include advertising pixels or analytics scripts. Stripe and other linked services have their own cookie and storage practices.

Use, access, and retention

We use the information described above to handle requests, supply agreed services, manage billing, resolve problems, maintain business records, and meet applicable legal obligations. Information is shared with the service providers needed for those purposes and may be disclosed where legally required.

We keep records for as long as needed for the relevant request or customer relationship, accounting and legal obligations, and resolving disputes. The appropriate period depends on the type of record; our service providers also maintain records under their policies.

Your requests

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete information we hold about you by emailing ryleechapin@kytherly.com. We may need to verify your identity. Some records may need to be retained for legal, accounting, or dispute-resolution purposes. If you believe we handled a request incorrectly, reply to ask Rylee Chapin to review it. Any additional rights depend on applicable law.

Information handled for your business

This policy does not describe every possible customer deployment. If you purchase messaging, phone, AI receptionist, or other services that handle information about your customers, the providers, data flows, permissions, and retention arrangements must be confirmed in the service-specific onboarding before activation.

Changes

We update this page when the practices described here change and revise the effective date. Material changes to an existing service relationship will be communicated as required by the applicable agreement or law.
